September 2026–present | Team 30 · Carbon Negative Solutions

Exploring a specialized small-language-model assistant grounded in steel-slag research. The central question is when a material-performance prediction can be trusted, especially when a new slag differs from the model’s training data.

Bias in, Bias Out? Auditing Bias Transfer in LLM Distillation

FME Project Planning | Columbia University

A benchmark study asking whether distilling a large language-model judge into a smaller one transfers or changes its position, length, and self-preference biases.

Research question and study design
Question: Does a smaller judge inherit a teacher’s systematic preferences—or can a bias-aware distillation recipe reduce them?
Planned comparison: Audit position, response-length, and model-family self-preference, with controlled bias injection and quality-aware controls.
Scope: This is a project plan; results and novelty claims have not been established.

Loom AI — Multi-Agent Calendar Planning Agent

January 2026 - May 2026 | New York, NY

A compact multi-agent planner that turns a one-line goal into validated, scheduled plans. LLMs handle language (decomposition, critique, rationale) while deterministic Python handles scheduling and validation for correctness.

Agent Orchestration: Multi-node LangGraph agent coordinating tool-calls and deterministic schedulers for reliable planning.
Hybrid Design: LLMs do decomposition, critique, and rationale; deterministic Python enforces time geometry, validation, and calendar writes to prevent hallucinated or invalid schedules.
Scheduling Strategies: Implements three heuristics—deadline-first, min-fragmentation, and energy-aware—to offer diverse valid plans.
Safety & UX: Checkpointed human approval prevents automatic calendar edits; deterministic validators check overlap, out-of-hours, and deadline violations.

Benchmarking KV-Cache Management for LLM Inference

January 2026 - May 2026 | New York, NY

A unified benchmark of KV-cache strategies for long-context LLM inference. Measured memory, latency, and quality across single-request and concurrent workloads to find practical trade-offs.

Throughput Harness: 500-request Poisson trace replay on vLLM to simulate heterogeneous concurrent traffic and stress KV memory.
Findings: Identified KV memory as the dominant throughput bottleneck (observed utilization 18.7% → 79.7%). v0-eager delivered best single-GPU throughput and lowest tail latency among configs tested.
Quality vs Memory: StreamingLLM can reduce KV memory dramatically on short tasks (up to ~198×) while PagedAttention preserves full-cache quality on long-form generation.
Reproducibility: Experiments run on Qwen2.5-7B with fixed seeds, pinned package versions, and committed per-run JSON artifacts for analysis.
